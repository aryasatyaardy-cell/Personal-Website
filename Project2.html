Explore the Theme That I Choose: Net Zero

Here the examinee wanted you to explore one of the theme that you choose deeply by asking yourself these questions

How?

By making a product that aligns with net zero movement such as biodegradability, sustainibility, or emission-aware products

Why?

Net zero movement is based on the goal to cut off carbon emmission by 2050, by balancing the production of carbon and the removal of carbon from our athmosphere, in order to sustain the earth

Where?

At home

- Detergent/soap or chemical damaging and polluting the environment

- Electricity source is non renewable (such as using fossil fuel to produce electricity or power)

- Buildings is being made by diessel powered machinery

At school

Lunch generate lots of food and packaging waste, with plastic utensils added

When?

- Cow's burb release a concerning amount of methane into the athmosphere

- Diesel/fossil fuels generate harmful CO2 emission

- Unrenewable source of electricity

Who?

I'm targeting the govemrent, enviromental acitivist, already developed country, and anyone who wants to contribute to this movement.

What's the problem?

Enviromental pollution

What's the solution?

A product to mitigate or reducing civillian waste or to process waste effeciently
