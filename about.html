About Me

I’m Asta (also known as Arya), a 16 years old student and aspiring engineer interested in turning ideas into practical technology.

My interests span engineering design, robotics, embedded systems, and marine technology. I enjoy working across both hardware and software—from designing mechanical systems and building prototypes to programming microcontrollers and developing the systems that connect everything together.

Many of my projects start as questions: Could this be built differently? Could it be more efficient? Could technology make this problem easier to solve? I use experimentation, iteration, and problem-solving to turn those questions into working prototypes.

My current work includes projects involving IoT, robotics, sensors, embedded systems, and mechanical design. One of my main projects is Shadow Hunter, a quadruped robot designed to collect environmental data in disaster-affected areas while minimizing the need to expose human responders to potential hazards.

I’m particularly interested in engineering that has a real-world purpose, especially technology that can contribute to disaster response, sustainable development, and the exploration of Indonesia’s marine environment.

This website documents the projects I’ve built, the problems behind them, and what I’ve learned along the way.
