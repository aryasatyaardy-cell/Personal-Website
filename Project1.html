Project #001

Pewter Casting

Design Requirements

Design Brief:

"Design a bespoke low-cost necklace pendant which can be worn by a male or female. The design must be a 2d shape and be manufactured using pewter metal. The pendant must be suitable for everyday wear and not exceed the measurements of 35mmx35mm. The pendant must have an attachment for a chain or necklace."

Client Information:

The client is a person who likes simple Jewellery design. The client has expressed interest in the themes of nature, architecture and cultural design. The design must be suitable for everyday wear and can include a small gemstone.

Criteria from the given brief

Aesthetic: Simple, nature themed pendant

Client: Preffer a simple jewelery

Dimension (LWH): 35mm x 2mm x 35mm

Material: Pewter metal

Manufacturing Process:Pewter casting

Client's Wants and Needs

My client wants a simple nature themed pendant. After speaking with the client, they want to use it as a necklace pendant. My client want to use the necklace pendant as an accessories on everyday life. Therefore, it has to be lightweight and simple pendant with a single small gemstones
